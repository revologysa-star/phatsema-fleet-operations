Training Guide

Dashboard: Working now is Working machines divided by active fleet. Operational is Working + Available divided by active fleet. These values recalculate after every refresh and status change.

Breakdown: Report the problem, assign an employee and optionally attach evidence. When the repair is complete, the assigned employee or manager confirms whether the machine is ready. Yes returns it to Working; No keeps it on Breakdown and records the reason.

Service: Search for a machine, enter the work completed and a current or higher hour reading. Evidence can be attached where required.
